Welcome

The British Values Hub celebrates the principles that shape life in the United Kingdom: democracy, the rule of law, individual liberty, mutual respect, and tolerance of different faiths and beliefs.

This site is designed for students, teachers, and visitors who want to understand what these values mean in practice and how they influence our communities, schools, and everyday life.

Core British Values

  • Democracy: Everyone has a voice and a vote in how decisions are made.
  • Rule of Law: Laws protect people and are applied fairly to everyone.
  • Individual Liberty: People are free to make choices and express their views.
  • Mutual Respect: We treat others with dignity, even when we disagree.
  • Tolerance: We accept and value different faiths, beliefs, and cultures.

About British Values

British values have developed over many centuries, shaped by key moments such as the Magna Carta, the growth of Parliament, and the extension of voting rights. These events helped establish ideas of fairness, representation, and accountability.

Today, British values are promoted in schools, workplaces, and public institutions. They encourage everyone to:

  • Take part in discussions and decision-making.
  • Follow rules and understand why they exist.
  • Stand up for their rights and the rights of others.
  • Show respect to people from different backgrounds.
  • Challenge discrimination and prejudice.

By understanding these values, we can build communities that are safer, fairer, and more inclusive for everyone.
