Welcome

This website celebrates the core British values that shape life in the United Kingdom: democracy, the rule of law, individual liberty, mutual respect, and tolerance of different faiths and beliefs.

Core British Values

  • Democracy: Everyone has a voice and a vote.
  • Rule of Law: Laws protect people and are applied fairly.
  • Individual Liberty: Freedom to make choices and express views.
  • Mutual Respect: Respecting others, even when we disagree.
  • Tolerance: Accepting and valuing different faiths and beliefs.

Why These Values Matter

British values help create a society where people feel safe, included, and able to participate. Schools, communities, and institutions promote these principles to ensure everyone understands their rights and responsibilities.
